Connect Your Workshop

Connect your Smart Auto Care mobile app with your Workshop Manager dashboard.

How to Connect

  1. Open the Smart Auto Care mobile application.
  2. Login using your Workshop Manager account.
  3. Open the QR Pairing option in the mobile app.
  4. Scan the QR code displayed on this website.
  5. Wait for backend verification.
